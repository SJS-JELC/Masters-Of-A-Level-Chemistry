Choose an element, then tap to place it or drag from an atom to extend the structure. Tap a bond to change its order. Join existing atoms to close a ring. Hydrogens are added automatically; the H tool also lets you draw them explicitly.

Keyboard: C/O/N/F/H choose an element; L selects chlorine; 3 toggles Chain; E selects Erase. Tab reaches the canvas and atoms. Enter places or changes an atom; arrow keys on an atom preview a bond and Enter commits it. Delete erases, Ctrl+Z undoes, and Escape cancels a gesture.
