(c) The scheme below contains a number of mystery organic compounds R–Z. Draw the structures for the compounds R–Z.

Use the following hints to help you.

  • All the compounds R–Z contain only carbon, hydrogen and oxygen.
  • Each carbon atom in all of the molecules R–Z is bonded to exactly one other carbon atom.
  • There are no C=C (double) or C≡C (triple) bonds in any of the compounds.
  • It is usually more thermodynamically stable for a carbon to have a double bond to an oxygen atom than to have two single bonds to hydroxyl (OH) groups.
